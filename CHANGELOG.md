# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first release (Minecraft 1.21.1)

The first public release of Wild West Universe.

- **World:** western Overworld without oceans, snow or jungles; new biomes Red Mesa, Sagebrush Flats and Canyon River;
  taller plateaus and river canyons; oak savanna trees; sandy haze; tumbleweeds blown by the wind; Desert Skeleton.
- **Places:** western towns with a saloon plaza and a dozen building types, ranches, outlaw hideouts, wrecked
  stagecoaches, train stations and three wanted-boss hideouts.
- **Horses:** realistic animated horse replacing the vanilla one; walk, trot and gallop; stamina, hunger, horseshoes
  and hoof wear; sexes and inherited stamina; whistle; swimming; western tack visible on the horse; wild herds led by
  a stallion.
- **Wagons:** covered wagon and cargo cart pulled by your horse.
- **Weapons:** revolver, lever-action rifle and double-barrelled shotgun with their own ammunition, reload key and HUD;
  dynamite, throwing tomahawk and Bowie knife; 3D voxel models with animations.
- **Enemies:** outlaw gangs and leaders; wanted posters and three bosses (Black Jack, Dynamite Dan, The Butcher) with
  boss bars, gangs and unique rewards; three desert creepers.
- **Wildlife:** bison, scorpions, coyotes, cougars and vultures; animals leave carcasses.
- **Frontier life:** dollars and wallets; storekeeper, bartender, gunsmith, horse trader and sheriff shops;
  gunslinger duels; six pieces of western clothing with perks; satchels with their own slot; wineskin and beer;
  bedroll.
- **Railway:** drivable steam locomotive, passenger car and mail car on ordinary rails; train robbery.
- **Advancements:** the *Wild West* advancement tab.
